# mobile-eeg-artifact-benchmark

Code and per-participant results for:

> A reproducible, decoding-centred benchmark of lightweight IMU-referenced motion-artifact removal for ambulatory EEG-BCI, with a causal replay of motion-gated adaptive filtering. Yao Xie and Lirui Sun, 2026 (manuscript in preparation).

Everything in the paper's tables is regenerated by `code/make_tables.py` from the JSON files in `results/`. The scripts that produce those JSON files are listed below; they need the public datasets, which are **not** redistributed here.

## Environment

Python 3.11; `pip install -r requirements.txt` (versions pinned to those used for the paper). No GPU. Runtimes below are for 4 worker processes on an 8-core machine.

| 1. Retrospective benchmark, all methods | `python code/p1_clean_eval.py none,asr,asr10,reg,cca,gait,gait+reg 4 results_24` | `results/p1_clean_eval/results_24.json` | 15 min |
| 1b. Gated NLMS, step-size and threshold sweeps | `python code/p1_clean_eval.py none,nlms,nlms_gated 4 results_24_gated`; `… none,nlms_gated,nlms_mu02,nlms_mu10,nlms_mu20,cca20,cca40 4 results_24_tuning` | `results_24_gated.json`, `results_24_tuning.json` | 25 min |
| 1c. Rank-safe ASR and iCanClean-style windowed CCA | `python code/p1_clean_eval.py none,asr,asr10,icc,icc_w4 4 results_24_asrfix_icc` | `results_24_asrfix_icc.json` | 15 min |
| 1d. Sensor ablation and ridge penalty | `python code/p1_clean_eval.py none,reg,reg_head,reg_ankle,nlms 4 results_24_ablation`; `… none,reg_shift,nlms_shift,reg_a1,reg_a3 4 results_24_controls` | `results_24_ablation.json`, `results_24_controls.json` | 20 min |
| 1e. Surrogate-reference controls | `python code/p1_clean_eval.py none,reg,nlms_gated,reg_shift10,reg_shift120,reg_surr,nlms_shift10,nlms_shift120,nlms_surr 4 results_24_controls2` | `results_24_controls2.json` | 25 min |

| 3. Causal replay (gate threshold 50/100/200) | `python code/p1_causal_replay.py 4 100 0.05` (and 50, 200) | `results/p1_causal_replay/results_thr*_mu0.05.json` | 10 min each |

Method names used on the command line (see the docstring of `code/p1_clean_eval.py`): `none`, `reg` (lagged IMU ridge regression), `cca`/`cca20`/`cca40` (global IMU-referenced CCA, r threshold 0.3/0.2/0.4), `icc`/`icc_w4` (iCanClean-style windowed CCA, 2 s / 4 s), `gait`, `gait+reg`, `asr`/`asr10` (rank-safe ASR, cutoff 20/10), `nlms`, `nlms_gated`, `nlms_mu02/10/20`, `reg_head`, `reg_ankle`, `reg_a1`/`reg_a3` (ridge 0.01 %/1 %), `reg_shift`/`reg_shift10`/`reg_shift120`/`reg_surr` and `nlms_shift*`/`nlms_surr` (surrogate references).

## What is in `results/`

- `p1_clean_eval/*.json` — per-participant decoding performance (`per_subject`) and condition means (`summary`) for every retrospective run; `stats_primary.*`, `heldout_param_selection.json`, `timing.json`, `imu_only_decoding.json`.
- `p1_causal_replay/results_thr{50,100,200}_mu0.05.json` — causal replay per participant, per-second gate decisions per segment, transition metrics; `heldout_gate_threshold.json`.
- `tables_v2.md` / `tables_v2.json` — every table in the paper, generated from the above.
- `mobile_bci_baseline/`, `wrist_exo_baseline/`, `wrist_encoder_reference/` — original-protocol baselines and the Supplement S1 experiment.

## Notes that matter for reproduction

- The two releases of the Mobile BCI dataset differ: figshare is 500 Hz raw (18 participants); OSF is 100 Hz with integer-quantized IMU channels (standing yields constant values). The causal gate's calibration denominator is floored at 1e-30 for this reason.
- Common-average referencing plus bad-channel interpolation makes the EEG covariance rank-deficient; ASR applied directly diverges on several OSF participants. `m_asr` projects onto the full-rank calibration subspace first.
- Participant `sub-21` has no IMU channels in its standing ERP file and is excluded from standing ERP everywhere (`make_tables.py` enforces this).
- Held-out folds use seed 20261004 (`heldout_param_selection.py`); fold identities are stored in its output.
